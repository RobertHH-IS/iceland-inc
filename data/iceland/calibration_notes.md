## Weakest values and known gaps

- Home ownership by age: latest age-specific data is EU-SILC 2016 (Hagstofa LIF03211), measured as persons living in owner-occupied dwellings. It overstates ownership among the young. No register-based ownership rate by age was found.
- Import content: OECD TiVA only runs to 2022. The household figure is a proxy based on total final consumption, including government.
- Profits to foreign owners: this is income on all inward FDI, not smelters alone, and it swings a lot (2023 1.98%, 2024 1.25%, 2025 -0.02% of GDP).
- Age-band splits in TEK02011 and the LFS assume a uniform distribution within 5-year bands (65-69 split 2/5 to working and 3/5 to old; 15-19 halved). This biases wages and employment of the 67+ group upward.
- Household assets, debts and incomes (THJ09000/1) are per tax family, classified by the age of the head of the family, and not by the age of each person.
- Corporate debt: the CBI concept (75.5%) and the non-consolidated financial-accounts concept (131%) differ widely. Pick according to the model's sector definitions.
- Government COFOG year is 2025 (latest; 2024 is distorted by Grindavik support). 2024 values are given in each note.
- Firm sectors: the value added and pay of other exporters (pharma, data centres, software, freight, business services) are estimates: other exports times the value-added and labour shares of the industries that make them. Tourism's pay is an estimate from TSA employment and average pay in its main industries. The register gives no 18-34 age split by industry, so tourism's young workforce is proxied by the share of workers outside ages 25-64. Aluminium's value added is for all of basic metals (including the ferrosilicon and silicon plants) in 2025, a weak year for the smelters; its imported-input share counts all imported carbon electrodes, some of which go to the silicon plants.
- Bank loans by sector: only construction's share (17.7%) is published in the Financial Stability chart data; the other sector shares in the model are placeholders.
